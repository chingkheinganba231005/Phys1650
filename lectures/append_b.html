Notes ：
 Position

"N" (North) or "S" (South) denotes a constellation is mainly or wholly situated above +35° or below -35°  in declination respectively. "CE" (Celestial Equator) indicates that a constellation lies within ± 35° in declination.

Season

"Sp", "Su", "Fa" and "Wi" stand for "Spring", "Summer", "Fall" and "Winter" respectively. Season of a constellation is classified according to right ascension of the centre of a constellation, where right ascension ranging from 9hr to 15hr is spring constellation, 15hr to 21hr is summer constellation and vise versa. All constellations with declination -67° or below will not be classified.
