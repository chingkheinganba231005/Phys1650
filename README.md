# Nature of the Universe (PHYS1650)

Lecture notes for the introductory astronomy general education course
"Nature of the Universe", Department of Physics, The University of Hong Kong.

The site is published with GitHub Pages: https://chingkheinganba231005.github.io/Phys1650/

## Contents

- `index.html` — table of contents
- `lectures/` — 19 chapters plus 2 appendices
- `lectures/images/`, `images/` — figures and photos
