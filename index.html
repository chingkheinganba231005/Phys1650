The Department of Physics of The University of Hong Kong offers the general education course of introductory astronomy, with course title "Nature of the Universe". These are the lecture notes of those courses. The Chinese version was translated by The Hong Kong Space Museum. (Sorry, not available.) No prior knowledge in astronomy, physics or higher mathematics is required to understand these notes.

In the first part of the notes, we focus on the observational aspect of astronomy and our solar system. In the second part, we will discuss our own Sun, stars and their evolution, galaxies and cosmology.
